# Demo Recording Checklist

## Before recording

- Open the hosted root URL in a clean browser tab.
- Confirm the header says `Cloud Connected`.
- Confirm Workflow evidence says `Firestore persisted` and `Gemini 3.5 Flash`.
- Use a 16:9 capture at 1440p or 1080p; browser zoom 100%.
- Close notifications and unrelated tabs.
- Do not display Cloud credentials, API keys, personal data, or real veterinary records.

## One-click proof path

1. Click `Run guided demo` once.
2. Show 11 typed tasks appear in the Day 1–Day 7 timeline.
3. Show Gemini structure stool=5, appetite=normal, vomiting=false.
4. Show `REQUEST_OWNER_FOLLOW_UP` actions.
5. Show the VetBrief timeline, outstanding tasks, adherence, and `CONTINUE_MONITORING`.
6. Click `Download VetBrief` and mention that it contains the backend-rendered handoff.

## Acceptance

- Final notice: `Guided demo complete`.
- No error or stopped state.
- Safety disclaimer remains visible or is explicitly shown before closing.
- Architecture and live-proof evidence are shown after the product workflow, not instead of it.
